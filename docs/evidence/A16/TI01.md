# TI01: passenger and driver, from the stop list to the announcement

## Scenario

The whole passenger path against the laboratory:
1. The app downloads the stops.
2. The passenger asks for assistance.
3. The driver's HMI acknowledges the request.
4. The driver accepts it.
5. The passenger is told according to the phone context (C09): text and vibration with the phone in hand, voice with the phone covered.

The run also checks three announcement rules from #105:
- a decision survives the process being killed;
- only the newest decision of a request is announced;
- the app does not speak over TalkBack.

## Configuration

- **Code:** branch `feature/app-cleanup` at `ba4aabd` (#105, on top of `develop` with C11 and #103). Debug build against the laboratory:
  `./gradlew assembleDebug -PuseSampleApi=false -PapiBaseUrl=http://127.0.0.1:8000/ -PmqttBroker=tcp://127.0.0.1:11883`.
- **Laboratory:** API, PostgreSQL, Mosquitto and Node-RED on the VM, branch `feature/dist-cleanup` (#106). Demo reset before the run (trip `c49bf20c`).
- **Passenger:** Aquaris C, Android 8.1, real light and proximity sensors, voice mode AUTO (the default). App data cleared before the run. API and broker over USB (`adb reverse` and an SSH tunnel to the VM); Wi-Fi only for the CRTM download.
- **Driver:** HMI on a Pixel 8 emulator, same build and same tunnel.
- **Run:** by A on 23 Nov 2026, with the app in English. B and C were not present; C reviews the sheet.

## Announcement checks (#105)

The decisions already announced are stored in `shared_prefs/announced_decisions.xml` as `requestId:version`. The app is a debug build, so `adb shell run-as` can read that file.

| Check | How | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A decision survives the process being killed | Request at stop 461. The passenger app goes to the background, the driver accepts, and the process is killed with `run-as … kill -9`. Then the app is opened again | While hidden, nothing was announced and `70e015cc:2` was not on disk. On reopening, `/sync` brought the decision back. It was announced once and stored. The decision of step 4 (`421e6c33:2`) was not repeated |
| Only the newest decision of a request | Request at stop 456. The passenger app goes to the background, the driver accepts it (version 2) and then ends the trip (version 3). Then the app is opened again | Four decisions were processed, one per open request, all "trip ended". For stop 456 only `4c9676bd:3` was stored, never `4c9676bd:2`: the acceptance was not announced after the trip had ended |
| No speech over TalkBack | New demo trip, voice mode ALWAYS, TalkBack on. Request at stop 459, and the driver accepts | The message "The driver has accepted your request." was shown on screen for TalkBack to read. The app requested no audio focus; the only speech was TalkBack's own voice (`en-GB`, not the app's `en-US`) |

Images: `09-dedupe-trip-ended.png` (stop 456 after returning) and `10-talkback-text.png`.

Afterwards the phone was restored: TalkBack off, voice mode AUTO, screen timeout 1 minute, `adb reverse` removed.

## Result

**Passed.** The run covered the download, the request, the receipt, the acceptance and the announcement by context, with the real sensors of the phone and the app in English. The three announcement rules of #105 also hold on the device.

## Observations

- The phone's time zone is Europe/London and its clock is one hour ahead. So "Registered at 23:21" does not match the clock in the status bar. The app converts the server time correctly; the phone is misconfigured.
- After the process is killed, Tracking shows "Stop 461" instead of the stop name until the stop list loads again. This is minor, and it also happened before #105.
- After a demo reset, the HMI kept showing "Trip ended" until the driver tapped Refresh. The passenger side picked up the new trip.
